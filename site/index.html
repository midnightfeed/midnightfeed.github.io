Academy Museum unveils The Horror Show, with a John Carpenter retrospective and a 50th-anniversary Carrie

The museum's horror exhibition arrives with a full Carpenter series attached — the kind of institutional attention the genre's 1970s and '80s run has spent fifty years not getting.

    The Keeper's Desk

    Hammer's uncut Dracula collector's edition

    Hammer's Limited Collector's Edition of Dracula (1958) was scheduled for October 26, Dread Central reported: seven discs (two 4K UHD, five Blu-ray) carrying five versions of the film, including the uncensored UK cut rebuilt from Japanese reels found in Warner Bros.' archive, plus a 200-page book and a 60-page House of Hammer comic reprint.

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: Dread Central

    Ryū Murakami, author of Audition, dies at 74

    Rue Morgue reports that Ryū Murakami, author of Audition and In the Miso Soup, has died at 74. No cause was given. Audition (1997) became Takashi Miike's 1999 film.

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: Rue Morgue

    Tom Skerritt, Captain Dallas of Alien, dies at 93

    Rue Morgue reports that Tom Skerritt, who played Captain Dallas in Alien (1979), has died at 93. His other genre credits include The Dead Zone (1983), Poltergeist III (1988) and the TV film Stephen King's Desperation (2006).

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: Rue Morgue

    McCammon and Lansdale named StokerCon 27 Special Guests

    Robert McCammon and Joe R. Lansdale will be the Special Guests at the Horror Writers Association's StokerCon 27, McCammon's site announced on October 20.

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: RobertMcCammon.com

    Fright-Rags launches Tales from the Morgue

    Fright-Rags has launched an original horror anthology comic, Fright-Rags Presents: Tales from the Morgue, published with American Mythology and hosted by the company's ghoul, the Fantom. Midtown Comics lists the one-shot for November 4, with a Century Black & White cover by Roy Allan Martinez limited to 100 copies.

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: Bloody Disgusting

    Lugosi's Dracula cape at the Academy Museum

    The Horror Show at the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures in Los Angeles runs through July 25, 2027. On display: Bela Lugosi's restored cape from Dracula (1931), an original Creature from the Black Lagoon mask and storyboards from Psycho (1960).

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: Haunted Attraction Network

    Four new Blu-rays from Mondo Macabro

    Mondo Macabro will issue standard Blu-ray editions of The Satin Spider (1986), Bo Ba Bu (1998), Lust in the Swamps / Secrets of Sinful Athens (1966) and Memoria (1976), Blu-ray.com reports. Horror Society lists Memoria for December 8.

    Doug Graves · 26 Oct · Source: Blu-ray.com
